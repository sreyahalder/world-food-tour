Method: built-in image_gen imagegen tool, original generated PNG preserved.

Reference: user's pixel food icon sheet, plus their uploaded Albanian mixed-grill and soup photos.
Layout: four columns, three rows, eleven dishes and an empty plate fallback.
Order: Kabuli pulao, mantu, bolani, byrek; tavë kosi, fërgesë, couscous, chakhchoukha; mhadjeb, Albanian mixed grill, Albanian soup, plate.

The app reads measured crop coordinates from lib/dish-sprite-atlas.ts and crops each sprite into a canvas at its original resolution. Color and alpha are preserved, with no additional pixelation or opacity threshold. Normal browser scaling fits the artwork into a 64-pixel sticker, partly overlapping the photo's top-left corner. The original atlas is not modified. There is no badge backing or CSS shadow. The older dish-sprites-v2-rendered.png contact sheet documents the superseded 32-pixel rendering.

Existing SVG assets are retained as v1 references. The nine cuisine-guide entries and four uploaded NYC dishes use the new atlas. Known names are assigned automatically on upload. An unrecognized dish uses the transparent empty-plate placeholder until a matching illustration is added. New photo uploads are no longer converted into pixel-photo badges.

Match the original reference image's LOW RESOLUTION 32x32 pixel art exactly in spirit: small pixel clusters, 1-pixel outline, modest pixel detail, 12–20 colors per sprite, 3 shades per material, warm ochre/brown highlights and cream plate pixels.
